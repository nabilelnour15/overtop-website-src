# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Four audiences, all business buyers arriving to judge whether Overtop can move or clear their cargo:

- **Egyptian exporters and factories** (for example a copper wire rod producer) who need export declarations, certificates, packing, lashing, booking and shipping out of any Egyptian port or airport.
- **Egyptian importers and traders** who need goods cleared at port, duties settled and cargo delivered to their warehouse.
- **Overseas buyers and suppliers** sourcing from Egypt or shipping to Egyptian clients, who read the site in English and need to trust a counterpart they have never met.
- **Companies needing customs advice** before they ship: classification, duties, procedures and documents.

Visitors are in a work context, often on a phone, often Arabic-first. The job is to understand what Overtop covers and get a quote, by form, call or email.

## Product Purpose

Overtop is an Egyptian company, operating since 2017, in export, import, transport and logistics, and customs services (including customs consulting). The site presents those services and turns a visit into a quote request. Success is a qualified quote request: a name, a phone number and a clear description of the cargo.

## Positioning

One accountable team for the whole chain (paperwork, port and road), working through every Egyptian port and airport, by sea and air, with one contact and a clear price before work starts. Not tied to a single port.

## Operating Context

- Services: Import; Export (with copper wire rod 8 mm, EN 12166, as the featured product); Transport & Logistics (door to door, port to door, door to port, cargo handling, containers, packing, lashing, sea, air and road); Custom Services (consulting, classification, declarations, inspection, release).
- Quotes arrive through the form (opens the visitor's email app), the phone numbers and the info email. There is no backend.
- Egyptian ports named on the site: Port Said, Damietta, Ain Sokhna, Safaga; Cairo Airport for air. The office is at 68 El Houria Ave, Alexandria, but the company must never read as Alexandria-only.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static one-page site, English and Arabic (RTL), built with Vite, React, TypeScript and Tailwind, prerendered and published to GitHub Pages from a private source repo.
- Both languages must always stay in step; a missing translation is a type error.
- Open decisions: the EN 12166 standard for the copper product is to be confirmed against the factory certificate (8 mm rod is usually specified to EN 1977); the copper specs on the site (Cu-ETP, 99.90% Cu min., coils on pallets) are typical values, not confirmed company figures.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Overtop / أوفرتوب. Logos and mark in `Logo files/` and `website/public/brand/`.
- Signal red (#DC0023) is the single brand accent; the red triangle is part of the identity.
- Photography direction: modern, clean, orderly Egyptian sea and air logistics.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real: company facts in `OVERTOP LETTER.pdf`, phone numbers, email, address, "since 2017", the service list and the generated photography in `images/`.
- Absent, and must not be fabricated: client names, testimonials, shipment counts, fleet size, tonnage, prices, licences, certifications or trade-body memberships.

## Product Principles

1. Say what Overtop actually covers, plainly and in both languages; no claims without a source.
2. Make the next step obvious: a quote request is never more than one tap away, on phone too.
3. Show the whole chain, every Egyptian port and both sea and air, rather than a single port or service.
4. Earn trust from clarity (clear scope, clear documents, a named contact), not from invented proof.
5. Arabic and English are equal: neither is a translation afterthought.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for WCAG 2.2 AA. Arabic must be fully right-to-left. Respect reduced motion (the globe and the shipment line already do). Usable on small phones and slow connections.
